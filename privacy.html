Privacy Policy

We keep your data private by design.

SecureVault encrypts files in your browser, then saves only the encrypted output to Firestore. We do not store your secret key, and we cannot decrypt your files for you.

What data we collect

We collect your email address only for authentication. The files you upload are encrypted locally before they are stored in Firestore, and the secret key used for encryption is never transmitted or saved.

SecureVault does not have access to your decrypted files. If you lose your secret key, the encrypted files cannot be recovered.
